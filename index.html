Swiflly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short: Swiflly turns your speech into text entirely on your own computer. It never saves audio and does not collect any personal data. What you dictate is not stored; text is saved only if you start meeting notes or save a transcript, and then only on your PC. Translation, clean-up, the writing tools and recording transcription also run on your PC. Swiflly uses the internet only to download a model you choose.

What Swiflly does

Swiflly is a voice-typing app for Windows. When you press Ctrl+Win, it listens through your microphone. When you press Ctrl+Win again, it converts your speech to text and types it into the app you are using.

Your voice and your words

  • Processed on your device only. Speech recognition runs locally on your PC. Your audio is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Never saved. Audio is held in memory only while you are dictating and is discarded as soon as the text has been typed. It is never written to disk.
  • Text is not stored. Swiflly does not save what you dictate. The text is placed on the Windows clipboard for a moment so it can be pasted, and your previous clipboard text is then restored.
  • Recent dictations stay in memory. If "Recent dictations" is on (Dictation page), your last 10 dictations are kept in memory so you can copy one again from Swiflly's tray menu. They are never written to disk and are gone when Swiflly closes. You can clear the list or turn it off at any time.

Translation, clean-up and styles

Typing another language in English, Hinglish (Hindi in English letters), leaving out filler words like "um", the voice commands "new line", "new paragraph" and "scratch that", and the Polished and Formal styles all happen on your PC. The Polished and Formal styles use the writing model on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Snippets and app settings

Snippets you create (a phrase and the text it types) and the languages and styles you choose for particular apps are saved in Swiflly's settings on your PC. To apply an app's settings, Swiflly checks which program is in front when you start dictating (for example "whatsapp.exe"); it does not read that program's windows or content.

Meeting notes (optional)

  • Only when you start them. Meeting notes never start on their own. Each time, you confirm that you have told the other participants, and you are responsible for following the recording and consent laws that apply where you and they are.
  • What is captured. Your microphone (written as "You") and the sound your PC plays, such as the other people in a call (written as "Others"), are turned into text on your PC.
  • Text only, never audio. Audio is discarded as soon as each part is transcribed. The text is saved as a .txt file in your Documents\Swiflly Transcripts folder. You can open, move or delete these files at any time.
  • Summaries are your choice. Swiflly can copy the notes for you to paste into an AI assistant you choose. Swiflly itself never uploads them; once you paste them into another service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Writing tools (optional)

If you select text in an app and press Win+Alt+T (improve the English) or Win+Alt+P (turn it into a prompt for an AI assistant), Swiflly copies the selected text, rewrites it with a small language model that runs on your PC, and pastes the result over your selection. The text is never sent anywhere and is not kept: it lives in memory only while it is rewritten. Your clipboard is put back as it was.

Transcribing a recording (optional)

If you choose a recording in Swiflly's Transcribe page, Swiflly reads that file on your PC and turns its speech into text. The file is never uploaded, copied or changed, and its audio is discarded as soon as each part is transcribed. The text appears in the window; it is written to disk only if you click "Save as .txt" and choose where to save it.

Microphone

Swiflly uses the microphone only between your two Ctrl+Win presses, and during meeting notes you start. Windows shows its microphone indicator while Swiflly is listening. You can turn microphone access off at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.

Keyboard

To detect its shortcuts in every app, Swiflly listens for key presses. It only reacts to its two shortcuts (by default Ctrl+Win and Win+Alt for meeting notes; you can change them), and Esc and Enter while it is listening. It does not record, store or send anything you type. When your dictated text is ready, Swiflly asks Windows whether the focused item accepts text, so it can keep the text on the clipboard if it may not have arrived; this check does not read the content of anything. The writing tools read only the text you select, and only when you press their shortcut. To type, Swiflly sends Ctrl+V; when you say "scratch that", it sends Ctrl+Z to undo its last dictation.

Internet and data collection

  • Swiflly works offline. English is built in and needs no internet connection.
  • If you choose to dictate in another language, Swiflly downloads the speech model you pick from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This is a plain file download: nothing about you, your voice or your text is sent. After that, the language also works offline.
  • If you add your own model from a Hugging Face link, Swiflly downloads that file the same way. A model you add from your PC is simply copied into Swiflly's folder.
  • If you choose to use the writing tools, Swiflly downloads their language model (Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct) from Hugging Face the same way. After that, the writing tools also work offline.
  • "Send feedback" opens a new email to us in your email app, or in Gmail in your browser if no email app is set up. Nothing is attached or sent automatically: you write and send it yourself.
  • "Copy diagnostic info" (About page) copies technical details to your clipboard only: Windows version, processor, memory, microphone name, Swiflly's settings and its technical log, with your Windows user name taken out. Nothing is sent unless you paste it into an email yourself.
  • "Rate Swiflly", and once after 20 dictations, shows the Microsoft Store's own rating box. A rating or review you choose to give goes to Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Data stored on your PC

Swiflly keeps a few files in your Windows user profile: your settings (including your snippets, app settings and a count of your dictations, which is a number only, used to offer the rating box once), your optional list of custom words, any speech models you downloaded or added (you can remove them in the Languages window), the writing model if you downloaded it (removable in the Writing tools window), and a technical log of up to 1 MB (start-up and error messages, never audio and never the words you dictate). Uninstalling Swiflly removes them all. Meeting notes you chose to take, and transcripts you chose to save, stay where they were saved until you delete them.

Purchases

Swiflly is sold through the Microsoft Store. The free week and your yearly subscription are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Swiflly does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
